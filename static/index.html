04 / COVERAGE & LIMITS
A precise window. Not an all-seeing system.
01
Observed, not inferred
Live crawling covers bounded Ethereum block windows and supported transfer records. Internal calls, complete lifetime history, offchain accounts and other chains are outside this v1.
02
An address is not a person
Public labels and identifiers provide context. Vision does not identify individuals or turn transaction relationships into ownership claims.
03
No price, no conclusion
The sample reconciliation uses explicit synthetic sources and a fictional FIFO pack. Missing basis or price inputs must block a complete calculation, not become zero.
04
A research foundation
Before real casework: lawful data access, licensed feeds, approved jurisdiction rules, security controls, independent testing and accountable human review.
